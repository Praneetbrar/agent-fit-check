## Why agent readiness matters now

People are not only browsing. They launch **AI agents** to:

- research and compare offers  
- book appointments and reservations  
- buy products and services  
- ask support questions  
- check status, renew, cancel, or change plans  

A person will dismiss a cookie wall and try again. **An agent often will not.** It moves on — quietly — with the customer still attached to it.

That is not a demo trend. That is the next default way work gets done online.

### SEO vs AEO vs agent readiness

| Idea | What it optimized for | Gap today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SEO** | Humans and search crawlers finding pages | Ranking does not mean an agent can finish a job |
| **AEO** (answer engine optimization) | Being cited and chosen in AI answers | Visibility without a completable path still loses the conversion |
| **Agent readiness** | Agents finishing Discover → Decide → Transact → Support → Continue | Most sites still only work for patient humans who click |

**Agent Fit Check** measures the third layer — and gives you a concrete path to close it.

## The five pillars (Agent Fit model)

**Discover → Decide → Transact → Support → Continue**

| Pillar | The question | What usually breaks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Discover** | Can an agent find you and identify what you are? | Ambiguous branding, no clear entry, surfaces only after human-only navigation |
| **Decide** | Can it read offer, price, availability, and policies as facts? | Pricing in images, stock only known to staff, FAQs agents cannot treat as facts |
| **Transact** | Can it operate the path and finish — including auth when required? | Popup-only steps, dead-end wizards, “call us to finish,” blocked account flows |
| **Support** | Can it ask for help and get a clear answer or next step? | Help that only works for humans who can read a messy UI |
| **Continue** | Can it confirm, check status, change/cancel, or hand off cleanly? | No machine-clear confirmation, status, or lifecycle path |

Full methodology: [agentfitcheck.com/methodology](https://agentfitcheck.com/methodology)

## FAQ

### What is agent readiness?
Agent readiness means an AI agent can find your business, understand the offer, complete a real job (buy, book, ask, renew), and keep going — without a human finishing required steps mid-flow.

### What is Agent Fit Check?
Agent Fit Check is the product at [agentfitcheck.com](https://agentfitcheck.com) that scores agent readiness, gives a free fix prompt, and offers a $999 done-for-you pass for one journey.

### Is the score free?
Yes. The public check and fix prompt are free. No codebase and no card required for the score.

### What does $999 include?
One agreed primary journey under Transact, Support, or Continue (with Discover and Decide basics for that spine), on a supported stack, delivered as a pull request you control.

### How long does done-for-you take?
Typical turnaround is **7–21 days** after codebase access; outside cases up to **30 days** after access — see the service agreement for terms.

### Do you guarantee SEO rankings or traffic?
No. We score and improve whether agents can **finish the job** on the journey you choose. We do not sell ranking or traffic guarantees.

### How is this different from “adding AI to the homepage”?
Chat widgets and demos are not agent completability. Agents need clear facts, operable paths, and finishable checkout or support — not a polished hero for humans only.

### Can I DIY with the free prompt?
Yes — if you live in this stack. Review every change. If you do not, the safer path is the scoped $999 pass.

### What journeys can the $999 pass target?
Examples: buy, book, quote, ask a question, check status, cancel — one primary spine you choose under Transact, Support, or Continue.
